01 · PROBLEM DIAGNOSIS

The tourist acquisition problem

관광객 확보 문제

Olive Young wins the tourist in-store. The products are there, the brand recognition is there, the purchase happens. But the moment they leave Korea, Olive Young loses them permanently — because the entire loyalty and CRM infrastructure assumes a Korean resident.

I didn't find this problem in a report. I lived it. As a Malaysian Muslim in Seoul, I walked into Olive Young and couldn't sign up for their loyalty program — no Korean phone number required. That one friction point is the entire business case. 말레이시아 출신 무슬림으로 서울에서 직접 이 문제를 경험했습니다. 한국 번호가 없으면 멤버십 가입이 불가능합니다.

Four friction points — mapped from personal experience

📵
Korean number required
한국 번호 필수
Loyalty signup, Coupang Eats, most apps — locked behind a local number foreigners don't have.
📦
No in-store shipping home
해외 직배송 없음
Tourists self-limit basket size due to luggage. No option to ship purchases home at checkout.
🌐
Language barrier
언어 장벽
Staff and in-app content are Korea-first. International shoppers navigate product info alone.
👤
Zero data capture
데이터 미수집
Tourist buys, pays, and disappears. No profile, no email, no retargeting possible after departure.
Core diagnosis: Olive Young built a world-class in-store experience and a Korean-only digital backend. Every tourist who loves the brand has nowhere to go when they get home. 핵심 진단: 세계 수준의 매장 경험을 갖췄지만 디지털 백엔드는 한국인 전용입니다.

Query 1 Where does the loyalty funnel break for foreign customers?

외국인 고객의 로열티 퍼널 이탈 지점

SQL · PostgreSQL 
-- Loyalty signup funnel by customer nationality
-- Identifies drop-off at each step: foreign vs domestic

SELECT
  customer_type,
  COUNT(*) AS total_transactions,
  SUM(CASE WHEN loyalty_prompted  = TRUE THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS prompted,
  SUM(CASE WHEN signup_attempted  = TRUE THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS attempted,
  SUM(CASE WHEN signup_completed  = TRUE THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS completed,
  ROUND(
    SUM(CASE WHEN signup_completed = TRUE THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) * 100.0
    / NULLIF(COUNT(*), 0), 1
  ) AS completion_rate_pct
FROM   transactions
WHERE  transaction_date >= '2024-01-01'
GROUP BY customer_type
ORDER BY completion_rate_pct DESC;

Query 2 Which SKUs have the highest tourist purchase concentration?

관광객 구매 비중이 높은 SKU 식별

SQL · PostgreSQL 
-- Top SKUs by tourist purchase share vs domestic
-- Flags products with high tourist dependency = viral/reorder risk

SELECT
  p.sku_name,
  p.category,
  COUNT(CASE WHEN t.customer_type != 'domestic' THEN 1 END) AS tourist_orders,
  COUNT(*) AS total_orders,
  ROUND(
    COUNT(CASE WHEN t.customer_type != 'domestic' THEN 1 END) * 100.0
    / NULLIF(COUNT(*), 0), 1
  ) AS tourist_share_pct,
  ROUND(AVG(t.basket_size_krw)) AS avg_basket_krw
FROM   transactions t
JOIN   products p ON t.sku_id = p.sku_id
GROUP BY p.sku_name, p.category
HAVING tourist_share_pct > 30
ORDER BY tourist_share_pct DESC
LIMIT  6;

Query 3 How much recurring revenue is leaking annually?

연간 반복 매출 누수 규모 추정

SQL · PostgreSQL · CTE 
-- Revenue leakage estimate: tourists not retained in CRM
-- Assumes 25% retention, 6x annual reorders at avg ₩38,000

WITH tourist_base AS (
  SELECT
    customer_type,
    COUNT(DISTINCT customer_id)  AS unique_tourists,
    SUM(revenue_krw)              AS instore_revenue
  FROM   transactions
  WHERE  customer_type != 'domestic'
    AND  transaction_year = 2024
  GROUP BY customer_type
),
leakage AS (
  SELECT
    customer_type,
    unique_tourists,
    instore_revenue,
    ROUND(unique_tourists * 0.25)            AS recoverable_25pct,
    ROUND(unique_tourists * 0.25 * 6 * 38000) AS est_annual_recurring_krw
  FROM tourist_base
)
SELECT *,
  ROUND(est_annual_recurring_krw / instore_revenue * 100, 1)
    AS recurring_vs_instore_pct
FROM  leakage
ORDER BY est_annual_recurring_krw DESC;

04 · STRATEGIC RECOMMENDATION

Three interlocking solutions

세 가지 연동 솔루션

Each solution targets a different point in the tourist journey. Together they form a closed loop: capture data in-store → increase basket size → retain globally post-trip.

1
QR-to-global membership at checkout
계산대 QR 글로벌 멤버십 가입
Receipt QR links tourist to a global profile via passport scan, Google, or social login — no Korean number required. Creates a CRM profile that transitions to Olive Young's international e-commerce app after the tourist returns home. This is the data capture layer: without it, the other solutions have no customer to retain.
2
International counter + home postage service
국제 카운터 · 해외 직배송 서비스
Dedicated in-store counter at flagship locations where tourists ship purchases home directly. Removes the luggage constraint that self-limits basket size. Key insight from behavioural data: dopamine shopping peaks during travel — the willingness to buy more is high, but "how do I carry this?" is the blocker. Remove that friction and basket size increases. Estimated +22% basket uplift based on comparable duty-free shipping service data.
3
Post-trip retention loop + wishlist capture
귀국 후 재구매 유도 루프 · 위시리스트 수집
At QR scan, tourist is prompted to save a digital wishlist of items browsed but not purchased. Post-trip email sequence (Day 3 / Day 14 / Day 30) targets wishlist items with international shipping enabled. This converts browse intent into delayed purchase — addressing the "add-to-cart without buying" dopamine behaviour documented in recent e-commerce research. The wishlist becomes a retargeting seed that activates after departure and drives the first international order within 30 days. Expected conversion: 12–18% of wishlist captures to first international purchase.

Sample user stories — Olive Young global membership feature

사용자 스토리 샘플 · 올리브영 글로벌 멤버십 기능

Epic: Global CRM capture Must have
"As a foreign tourist at checkout, I want to create a membership profile using my Google account or passport ID, so that I can access Olive Young's loyalty benefits without a Korean phone number."
Acceptance criteria:
✓ User can scan QR on receipt to open signup flow
✓ Signup accepts Google OAuth, Apple ID, or passport number
✓ Korean phone number field is optional, not required
✓ Profile creation completes in under 90 seconds
✓ Confirmation email sent in user's detected language
Epic: In-store shipping Must have
"As a tourist purchasing multiple items, I want to ship my purchases directly to my home address from the store, so that I don't need to carry heavy bags through the airport."
Acceptance criteria:
✓ Shipping option available at all flagship store checkouts
✓ Supports addresses in 15+ countries
✓ Shipping fee calculated and displayed before confirmation
✓ Tracking number sent to membership email within 2 hours
✓ Estimated delivery date shown per destination country
Epic: Post-trip retention Should have
"As a returned tourist, I want to receive a personalised reorder reminder for products I purchased in-store, so that I can repurchase from my home country without searching again."
Acceptance criteria:
✓ Email triggered 14 days after checkout date
✓ Email contains exact products purchased with direct reorder links
✓ International shipping price shown in user's local currency
✓ Unsubscribe option clearly visible
✓ Email renders correctly in EN, ZH, MS, JA
