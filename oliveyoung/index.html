01 · PROBLEM DIAGNOSIS

The Tourist Acquisition Problem

관광객 확보 문제

Olive Young wins the tourist in store. The products, the brand recognition, the purchase. But the moment they leave Korea, Olive Young loses them "permanently".

Coming from my real experience as a tourist in Seoul before, I walked into Olive Young and couldn't sign up for their membership. No Korean phone number, no access. That single friction point is the entire business case.말레이시아 출신 여행자로 서울에서 직접 이 문제를 경험했습니다. 한국 번호가 없으면 멤버십 가입이 불가능합니다.

Query 1Where does the loyalty funnel break for foreign customers?

외국인 고객의 로여티 퍼널 이탈 지점

SQL · POSTGRESQL
-- Membership signup funnel by customer nationality
-- Identifies drop off at each step: foreign vs domestic

SELECT
  customer_type,
  COUNT(*) AS total_transactions,
  SUM(CASE WHEN loyalty_prompted = TRUE THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS prompted,
  SUM(CASE WHEN signup_attempted = TRUE THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS attempted,
  SUM(CASE WHEN signup_completed = TRUE THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS completed,
  ROUND(
    SUM(CASE WHEN signup_completed = TRUE THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) * 100.0
    / NULLIF(COUNT(*), 0), 1
  ) AS completion_rate_pct
FROM   transactions
WHERE  transaction_date >= '2024-01-01'
GROUP BY customer_type
ORDER BY completion_rate_pct DESC;

Query 2Which SKUs have the highest tourist purchase concentration?

관광객 구매 비중이 높은 SKU 식별

SQL · POSTGRESQL
-- Top SKUs by tourist vs domestic purchase split
-- Shows both sides of the comparison explicitly

SELECT
  p.sku_name, p.category,
  COUNT(CASE WHEN t.customer_type != 'domestic' THEN 1 END) AS tourist_orders,
  COUNT(CASE WHEN t.customer_type  = 'domestic' THEN 1 END) AS domestic_orders,
  COUNT(*) AS total_orders,
  ROUND(
    COUNT(CASE WHEN t.customer_type != 'domestic' THEN 1 END) * 100.0
    / NULLIF(COUNT(*), 0), 1
  ) AS tourist_share_pct,
  ROUND(
    COUNT(CASE WHEN t.customer_type  = 'domestic' THEN 1 END) * 100.0
    / NULLIF(COUNT(*), 0), 1
  ) AS domestic_share_pct,
  ROUND(AVG(CASE WHEN t.customer_type != 'domestic' THEN t.basket_size_krw END)) AS tourist_avg_basket_krw,
  ROUND(AVG(CASE WHEN t.customer_type  = 'domestic' THEN t.basket_size_krw END)) AS domestic_avg_basket_krw
FROM   transactions t
JOIN   products p ON t.sku_id = p.sku_id
GROUP BY p.sku_name, p.category
HAVING tourist_share_pct > 30
ORDER BY tourist_share_pct DESC
LIMIT 6;

Query 3How much recurring revenue is leaking annually?

연간 반복 매출 누수 규모 추정

SQL · POSTGRESQL · CTE
-- Revenue leakage: tourists not retained in CRM
-- Assumes 25% retention, 6x annual reorders at avg ₩38,000

WITH tourist_base AS (
  SELECT customer_type,
    COUNT(DISTINCT customer_id) AS unique_tourists,
    SUM(revenue_krw) AS instore_revenue
  FROM transactions
  WHERE customer_type != 'domestic'
    AND transaction_year = 2024
  GROUP BY customer_type
),
leakage AS (
  SELECT *,
    ROUND(unique_tourists * 0.25) AS recoverable_25pct,
    ROUND(unique_tourists * 0.25 * 6 * 38000) AS est_annual_recurring_krw
  FROM tourist_base
)
SELECT *,
  ROUND(est_annual_recurring_krw / instore_revenue * 100, 1) AS recurring_vs_instore_pct
FROM leakage
ORDER BY est_annual_recurring_krw DESC;

04 · STRATEGIC RECOMMENDATION

Three Interlocking Solutions

세 가지 연동 솔루션

Each solution targets a different point in the tourist journey. Together they form a closed loop: capture data in store → increase basket size → retain globally post trip.

1
QR to global membership at checkout
계산대 QR 글로벌 멤버십 가입
Receipt QR links tourist to a global profile via passport scan, Google, or social login — no Korean number required. Creates a CRM profile that transitions to Olive Young's international e-commerce app post return.
2
International counter + home postage service
국제 카운터 · 해외 직배송 서비스
Dedicated in store counter at flagship locations where tourists ship purchases home directly. Removes the luggage constraint that self limits basket size. Estimated +22% basket uplift based on comparable duty free shipping data.
3
Post trip retention loop + wishlist capture
귀국 후 재구매 유도 루프
At QR scan, tourist saves a digital wishlist. Post trip email sequence (Day 30 / Day 60 / Day 90) targets wishlist items with international shipping enabled. Expected conversion: 12–18% of wishlist captures to first international purchase.

ANALYST REFLECTION

My Thinking & Skills Applied

분석 과정과 적용된 역량

Why I chose this problem
이 문제를 선택한 이유
Most portfolio projects start from a dataset. This one started from a personal frustration. When I first came to Seoul as a tourist back in 2018, I noticed immediately that Korean retail infrastructure wasn't built for people like me. The Olive Young loyalty system was just one example of a broader pattern.

What made it interesting analytically is that this isn't a product problem or a brand problem. It's a systems and data architecture problem. The brand wins the customer in store and then has no mechanism to keep them.
Skills I applied and what I learned
적용한 역량과 배운 점

Funnel analysis thinking: At Delivery Hero I learned to ask "where exactly does the drop-off happen?" before jumping to solutions. That same discipline shapes Query 1 here.

SQL as a thinking tool: The three queries represent three different analytical lenses: funnel analysis (Query 1), segmentation (Query 2), and financial modelling (Query 3 with CTEs).

Demand forecasting method selection: The most important decision in forecasting is choosing the right method for the demand pattern. Applying ARIMA to a viral spike SKU like COSRX would produce meaningless forecasts.

Business analyst documentation (user stories): Writing user stories with proper acceptance criteria was not easy. The stories in "How I Work" are written to be implementable: a developer could read them and know exactly what to build.

What I would do differently: Validate funnel drop off rates with real Olive Young analytics. Run A/B tests on the QR signup flow at a single store before recommending full rollout.

My edge as an analyst for this problem
이 문제에 대한 나만의 강점
I am the real customer this analysis is about. I have walked into Olive Young as a foreign tourist, tried to sign up, failed, and left my data footprint nowhere. This gives me a pre-built intuition for which friction points matter most to the customer.
