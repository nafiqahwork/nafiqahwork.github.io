관광객 확보 문제

Olive Young wins the tourist in store. The products, the brand recognition, the purchase. But the moment they leave Korea, Olive Young loses them "permanently".

Query 1Where does the loyalty funnel break for foreign customers?

외국인 고객의 로열티 퍼널 이탈 지점

SQL · POSTGRESQL
-- Membership signup funnel by customer nationality
-- Identifies drop off at each step: foreign vs domestic

SELECT
  customer_type,
  COUNT(*) AS total_transactions,
  SUM(CASE WHEN loyalty_prompted = TRUE THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS prompted,
  SUM(CASE WHEN signup_attempted = TRUE THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS attempted,
  SUM(CASE WHEN signup_completed = TRUE THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS completed,
  ROUND(
    SUM(CASE WHEN signup_completed = TRUE THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) * 100.0
    / NULLIF(COUNT(*), 0), 1
  ) AS completion_rate_pct
FROM   transactions
WHERE  transaction_date >= '2024-01-01'
GROUP BY customer_type
ORDER BY completion_rate_pct DESC;

Query 2Which SKUs have the highest tourist purchase concentration?

관광객 구매 비중이 높은 SKU 식별

SQL · POSTGRESQL
-- Top SKUs by tourist purchase share vs domestic
-- Flags products with high tourist dependency

SELECT
  p.sku_name, p.category,
  COUNT(CASE WHEN t.customer_type != 'domestic' THEN 1 END) AS tourist_orders,
  COUNT(*) AS total_orders,
  ROUND(
    COUNT(CASE WHEN t.customer_type != 'domestic' THEN 1 END) * 100.0
    / NULLIF(COUNT(*), 0), 1
  ) AS tourist_share_pct,
  ROUND(AVG(t.basket_size_krw)) AS t_avg_basket_krw,
  COUNT(CASE WHEN t.customer_type = 'domestic' THEN 1 END) AS domestic_orders,
  ROUND(
    COUNT(CASE WHEN t.customer_type = 'domestic' THEN 1 END) * 100.0
    / NULLIF(COUNT(*), 0), 1
  ) AS domestic_share_pct,
  ROUND(AVG(t.basket_size_krw)) AS d_avg_basket_krw
FROM   transactions t
JOIN   products p ON t.sku_id = p.sku_id
GROUP BY p.sku_name, p.category
HAVING tourist_share_pct > 30
ORDER BY tourist_share_pct DESC
LIMIT 6;

Query 3How much recurring revenue is leaking annually?

연간 반복 매출 누수 규모 추정

SQL · POSTGRESQL · CTE
-- Revenue leakage: tourists not retained in CRM
-- Assumes 25% retention, 6x annual reorders at avg ₩38,000

WITH tourist_base AS (
  SELECT customer_type,
    COUNT(DISTINCT customer_id) AS unique_tourists,
    SUM(revenue_krw)             AS instore_revenue
  FROM transactions
  WHERE customer_type != 'domestic'
    AND transaction_year = 2024
  GROUP BY customer_type
),
leakage AS (
  SELECT *,
    ROUND(unique_tourists * 0.25)             AS recoverable_25pct,
    ROUND(unique_tourists * 0.25 * 6 * 38000) AS est_annual_recurring_krw
  FROM tourist_base
)
SELECT *,
  ROUND(est_annual_recurring_krw / instore_revenue * 100, 1) AS recurring_vs_instore_pct
FROM leakage
ORDER BY est_annual_recurring_krw DESC;

04 · STRATEGIC RECOMMENDATION

Three Interlocking Solutions

세 가지 연동 솔루션

Each solution targets a different point in the tourist journey and monetization. Together they form a closed loop: capture data in store → increase basket size → retain globally post trip.

1
QR to global membership at checkout
계산대 QR 글로벌 멤버십 가입
Receipt QR links tourist to a global profile via passport scan, Google, or social login which requires no Korean number. Creates a CRM profile that transitions to Olive Young's international e-commerce app post return. This is the data capture layer: without it, the other solutions have no customer to retain.
2
International counter + home postage service
국제 카운터 · 해외 직배송 서비스
Dedicated in store counter at flagship locations (Myeongdong, Hongdae, Sinchon) where tourists ship purchases home directly. Removes the luggage constraint that self limits basket size. Dopamine shopping peaks during travel: willingness to buy more is high, but "can i fit everything into my bag?" is the constraint. Estimated +22% basket uplift based on comparable duty free shipping data.
3
Post trip retention loop + wishlist capture
귀국 후 재구매 유도 루프 · 위시리스트 수집
At QR scan, tourist saves a digital wishlist of browsed but not purchased items. Post trip email sequence (Day 3 / Day 14 / Day 30) targets wishlist items with international shipping enabled. Converts browse intent into delayed purchase, which then addressing the "add-to-cart without buying" dopamine behaviour. Expected conversion: 12–18% of wishlist captures to first international purchase within 30 days.

ANALYST REFLECTION

My Thinking & Skills Applied

분석 과정과 적용된 역량

Why I chose this problem
이 문제를 선택한 이유
Most portfolio projects start from a dataset. This one started from a personal frustration. When I first came to Seoul as a tourist back in 2018, I noticed immediately that Korean retail infrastructure wasn't built for people like me; foreigner, non Korean speaking at first and without a local phone number. The Olive Young loyalty system was just one example of a broader pattern.

What made it interesting analytically is that this isn't a product problem or a brand problem. Olive Young's products are top tier and that's exactly why international tourists love them. It's a system(s) and data architecture problem. The brand wins the customer in store and then has no mechanism to keep them. That gap between in store success and zero post purchase relationship is where this business case lives.
Skills I applied and what I learned
적용한 역량과 배운 점

Funnel analysis thinking: I applied the waterfall thinking framework. At Delivery Hero I learned to ask "where exactly does the drop-off happen?" before jumping to solutions. That same discipline shapes Query 1 here — Foreigners don't sign up and so I traced the funnel step by step to identify which specific gate fails.

SQL as a thinking tool: Here, I try and test a lot. The three queries here aren't just code. They represent three different analytical lenses: funnel analysis (Query 1), segmentation (Query 2), and financial modelling (Query 3 with CTEs). I chose CTEs for Query 3 specifically because the leakage calculation has two logical stages which are tourist sizing, then revenue projection. (CTEs make that logic readable and auditable).

Demand forecasting method selection: The most important decision in forecasting is choosing the right method for the demand pattern (My professor, 2024). Applying ARIMA to a viral spike SKU like COSRX would produce meaningless forecasts. The method to pattern matching table in the "Forecasting" tab is the core analytical judgment.

Business analyst documentation (user stories): Writing user stories with proper acceptance criteria was not easy. "As a tourist, I want to sign up" is not a just any user story. The stories in the "How I Work" tab are written to be implementable: a developer could read them and know exactly what to build and how to test it.

What I would do differently with more time/data: I would validate the funnel drop off rates with real Olive Young app analytics. I would also run A/B tests on the QR signup flow at a single store before recommending full rollout. The 25% retention assumption is reasonable but unvalidated. The shipping counter basket uplift (+22%) relies on duty free analogues; a pilot at one Myeongdong store would give real lift data within 3 months.

My edge as an analyst for this problem
이 문제에 대한 나만의 강점
I am the real customer/user this analysis is about. I have walked into Olive Young as a foreign tourist, tried to sign up, failed, and left my data footprint nowhere. I had extra luggage payment worries upon arriving to Incheon Airport because there was no in store shipping option. I have navigated product labels in a language I was still learning.

This gives me a different experience: a pre-built intuition for which friction points matter most to the customer. The four pain points in the "Problem" tab are not the result of desk research. They are the result of lived experience, structured into a business framework.
