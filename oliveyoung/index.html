Query 1 Where does the loyalty funnel break for foreign customers? 외국인 고객의 로열티 퍼널 이탈 지점 SQL · PostgreSQL Copy -- Loyalty signup funnel by customer nationality -- Identifies drop-off at each step: foreign vs domestic SELECT customer_type, COUNT(*) AS total_transactions, SUM(CASE WHEN loyalty_prompted = TRUE THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS prompted, SUM(CASE WHEN signup_attempted = TRUE THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS attempted, SUM(CASE WHEN signup_completed = TRUE THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS completed, ROUND( SUM(CASE WHEN signup_completed = TRUE THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) * 100.0 / NULLIF(COUNT(*), 0), 1 ) AS completion_rate_pct FROM transactions WHERE transaction_date >= '2024-01-01' GROUP BY customer_type ORDER BY completion_rate_pct DESC; customer_type total_transactions prompted attempted completed completion_rate_pct Domestic (Korean) 284,500 241,825 198,150 181,280 63.7% Foreign (Japan/Taiwan) 48,200 40,970 18,716 7,230 15.0% Foreign (SEA) 31,400 26,690 8,478 942 3.0% Foreign (Western) 19,100 16,235 4,011 382 2.0% Loyalty signup completion rate by customer type Korean 63.7%, Japan/Taiwan 15%, SEA 3%, Western 2%. 💡 SEA tourists are prompted to sign up at the same rate as Korean customers — but only 3% complete it, vs 63.7% for Koreans. The drop-off is entirely at the "requires Korean phone number" step. The intent is there; the system kills it. 동남아 관광객 회원가입 완료율 3% — 의지가 없는 것이 아니라 한국 번호 요구 단계에서 이탈합니다. Query 2 Which SKUs have the highest tourist purchase concentration? 관광객 구매 비중이 높은 SKU 식별 SQL · PostgreSQL Copy -- Top SKUs by tourist purchase share vs domestic -- Flags products with high tourist dependency = viral/reorder risk SELECT p.sku_name, p.category, COUNT(CASE WHEN t.customer_type != 'domestic' THEN 1 END) AS tourist_orders, COUNT(*) AS total_orders, ROUND( COUNT(CASE WHEN t.customer_type != 'domestic' THEN 1 END) * 100.0 / NULLIF(COUNT(*), 0), 1 ) AS tourist_share_pct, ROUND(AVG(t.basket_size_krw)) AS avg_basket_krw FROM transactions t JOIN products p ON t.sku_id = p.sku_id GROUP BY p.sku_name, p.category HAVING tourist_share_pct > 30 ORDER BY tourist_share_pct DESC LIMIT 6; sku_name category tourist_orders total_orders tourist_share_pct avg_basket_krw COSRX Snail Serum 96 Serum 18,420 38,250 48.2% ₩42,800 Laneige Lip Sleeping Mask Lip care 22,100 46,500 47.5% ₩28,400 Some By Mi AHA BHA Toner Toner 12,300 31,800 38.7% ₩35,200 Dr. Jart Cicapair Cream Moisturiser 9,800 26,400 37.1% ₩58,600 Missha Time Revolution Serum 7,600 22,100 34.4% ₩51,200 Innisfree Green Tea Serum Serum 6,900 21,500 32.1% ₩39,800 Tourist purchase share by SKU (%) COSRX 48.2%, Laneige 47.5%, Some By Mi 38.7%, Dr Jart 37.1%, Missha 34.4%, Innisfree 32.1%. 💡 Nearly half of COSRX and Laneige Lip Mask sales are tourist-driven — yet none of these buyers are in the CRM. Query 3 How much recurring revenue is leaking annually? 연간 반복 매출 누수 규모 추정 SQL · PostgreSQL · CTE Copy -- Revenue leakage estimate: tourists not retained in CRM -- Assumes 25% retention, 6x annual reorders at avg ₩38,000 WITH tourist_base AS ( SELECT customer_type, COUNT(DISTINCT customer_id) AS unique_tourists, SUM(revenue_krw) AS instore_revenue FROM transactions WHERE customer_type != 'domestic' AND transaction_year = 2024 GROUP BY customer_type ), leakage AS ( SELECT customer_type, unique_tourists, instore_revenue, ROUND(unique_tourists * 0.25) AS recoverable_25pct, ROUND(unique_tourists * 0.25 * 6 * 38000) AS est_annual_recurring_krw FROM tourist_base ) SELECT *, ROUND(est_annual_recurring_krw / instore_revenue * 100, 1) AS recurring_vs_instore_pct FROM leakage ORDER BY est_annual_recurring_krw DESC; customer_type unique_tourists instore_revenue recoverable (25%) est. recurring/yr recurring vs instore Japan / Taiwan 48,200 ₩18.3B 12,050 ₩2.75B 15.0% SEA 31,400 ₩11.9B 7,850 ₩1.79B 15.0% Western 19,100 ₩7.3B 4,775 ₩1.09B 15.0% 💡 A conservative 25% retention rate — with just 6 annual reorders at avg ₩38,000 — translates to ₩5.6B in estimated annual recurring revenue currently uncaptured. Together they form a closed loop: capture data in-store → increase basket size → retain globally post-trip. 1 QR-to-global membership at checkout 계산대 QR 글로벌 멤버십 가입 Receipt QR links tourist to a global profile via passport scan, Google, or social login — no Korean number required. Creates a CRM profile that transitions to Olive Young's international e-commerce app after the tourist returns home. This is the data capture layer: without it, the other solutions have no customer to retain. 2 International counter + home postage service 국제 카운터 · 해외 직배송 서비스 Dedicated in-store counter at flagship locations where tourists ship purchases home directly. Removes the luggage constraint that self-limits basket size. Key insight from behavioural data: dopamine shopping peaks during travel — the willingness to buy more is high, but "how do I carry this?" is the blocker. Remove that friction and basket size increases. Estimated +22% basket uplift based on comparable duty-free shipping service data. 3 Post-trip retention loop + wishlist capture 귀국 후 재구매 유도 루프 · 위시리스트 수집 At QR scan, tourist is prompted to save a digital wishlist of items browsed but not purchased. Post-trip email sequence (Day 3 / Day 14 / Day 30) targets wishlist items with international shipping enabled. This converts browse intent into delayed purchase — addressing the "add-to-cart without buying" dopamine behaviour documented in recent e-commerce research. The wishlist becomes a retargeting seed that activates after departure and drives the first international order within 30 days. Expected conversion: 12–18% of wishlist captures to first international purchase.
